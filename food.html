Starters & Snacks


Samosa

A triangular pastry filled with spiced potatoes, peas, and sometimes meat.

Pakora (Bhajji)

A deep-fried fritter made from ingredients like onions, potatoes, spinach, or paneer, dipped in chickpea flour (besan) batter and fried until crispy.

Pani Puri (Puchka)

Hollow, crispy puris filled with spiced water, tamarind chutney, boiled potatoes, and chickpeas. Each bite is an explosion of flavors.

Dhokla

A savory steamed cake made from fermented rice and chickpea batter.

Vada Pav

A spicy potato filling encased in a gram flour batter and deep-fried, served inside a pav (bread bun), often with chutneys and fried green chilies.

Idli

Soft, steamed cakes made from fermented rice and urad dal (black gram) batter.

Paneer Tikka

Cubes of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) marinated in yogurt and spices, then grilled or baked to perfection.

Bhel Puri

A savory snack made with puffed rice, sev, peanuts, and a mix of vegetables like onions and tomatoes, tossed with tangy tamarind chutney.

Shakkarpara

Sweet, crispy, diamond-shaped fried dough, made with flour and sugar syrup.

Banana Chips

Thin slices of banana, deep-fried until crispy, and often flavored with salt or spices.

Moong Dal Chilla

A savory pancake made from ground moong dal (green gram) batter, often filled with vegetables or paneer.

Beverages


Masala Chai

A spiced tea made by brewing black tea with a mixture of aromatic spices like cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and ginger. Milk and sugar are added for a rich, comforting flavor.

Lassi

A refreshing yogurt-based drink that can be sweet or salty.

Aam Panna

A tangy and refreshing drink made from raw mangoes, flavored with spices like cumin, black salt, and mint. It's known for its cooling effect.

Thandai

A traditional cold drink made with a mixture of milk, sugar, almonds, fennel seeds, rose petals, pepper, cardamom, saffron, and sometimes bhang (cannabis leaves).

Nimbu Pani (Shikanji)

A refreshing lemonade made with freshly squeezed lemons, water, sugar, black salt, and spices like cumin.

Sol Kadhi

A tangy and refreshing drink made from kokum fruit and coconut milk, flavored with spices like cumin, mustard seeds, and garlic.

Neer Mor

A South Indian spiced buttermilk made with yogurt, water, green chilies, ginger, curry leaves, and cumin.

Kanji

A fermented drink made from black carrots, mustard seeds, and water. It has a tangy and spicy flavor. A popular accompaniment in Maharashtra and Rajasthan.

Sharbat

A refreshing, sweet drink made from a variety of fruits, flowers, and herbs like rose, khus (vetiver), sandalwood, and lime.

Kesar Pista Milk

A luxurious drink made with saffron (kesar), pistachios (pista), and milk, often sweetened and garnished with more nuts.

Chutneys & Pickles


Mint Chutney

Refreshing and tangy chutney made with fresh mint leaves, cilantro, and spices. Perfect with snacks like samosas and kebabs.

Coconut Chutney

Creamy chutney with grated coconut, roasted chana dal, and spices. A staple in South Indian cuisine, served with dosas and idlis.

Tamarind Chutney

Sweet and tangy chutney made with tamarind pulp, jaggery, and spices. A must-have condiment for chaat and samosas.

Mango Chutney

Sweet and spicy chutney made with ripe or raw mangoes, spices, and sugar. Popular throughout India, served with rice and parathas.

Peanut Chutney

Nutty and mildly spicy chutney made with roasted peanuts, garlic, and tamarind. Common in South Indian meals.

Garlic Chutney

Fiery chutney made with garlic, red chilies, and oil. A popular accompaniment in Maharashtra and Rajasthan.

Mango Pickle (Aam ka Achar)

One of the most popular pickles in India, made from raw mangoes marinated in a mix of spices like mustard seeds, fenugreek, red chili powder, turmeric, and salt.

Lemon Pickle (Nimbu ka Achar)

Made from lemons, this pickle is tangy and slightly bitter, often spiced with mustard seeds, fenugreek, and turmeric.

Green Chili Pickle

Made from green chilies that are marinated in spices like mustard seeds, fenugreek, and turmeric, and preserved in oil or lemon juice.

Mixed Vegetable Pickle

A combination of vegetables like carrots, cauliflower, turnips, and green chilies, marinated in spices and preserved in oil.

Gooseberry Pickle (Amla ka Achar)

Amla (Indian gooseberry) is marinated in spices like turmeric, mustard seeds, and chili powder, and preserved in oil.

Jackfruit Pickle (Kathal ka Achar)

Made from raw jackfruit, this pickle is marinated with spices like mustard seeds, fenugreek, turmeric, and chili powder, and preserved in oil.

Fish Pickle

A spicy and tangy pickle made from fish, marinated in a mixture of spices, vinegar, and oil.

Radish Pickle (Mooli ka Achar)

Fiery chutney made with garlic, red chilies, and oil. A popular accompaniment in Maharashtra and Rajasthan.

Mushroom Pickle

A unique pickle made from mushrooms, marinated in a mixture of spices and preserved in oil or vinegar.

Indian Herbs & Spices


Discover the medicinal and culinary uses of herbs found across India.


Turmeric

Known for its anti-inflammatory properties and vibrant color.

Holy Basil (Tulsi)

Promotes mental clarity, reduce stress, & support the immune system.

Coriander (Dhania)

Known for its cooling properties and is used to aid digestion.

Fenugreek (Methi)

Known to help regulate blood sugar levels and improve digestion.

Ashwagandha

Known for its ability to help the body manage stress, reduce anxiety & boost energy.

Ginger (Adrak)

It's commonly used to treat colds, nausea, and indigestion.

Cardamom (Elaichi)

Cardamom is a fragrant spice with a sweet and slightly peppery flavor. It is commonly used in Indian sweets, savory dishes, and chai (Indian spiced tea).

Indian Gooseberry (Amla)

It's considered a rejuvenating herb and is used to enhance vitality, boost immunity, and promote healthy skin and hair.

Neem

It's a bitter herb with strong antibacterial and antifungal properties.

Kesar

It has a distinctive aroma and imparts a rich golden color to dishes.

Curry Leaves

These are aromatic leaves used to add flavor to a variety of Indian dishes, especially in South Indian cuisine.

Licorice Rice (Mulethi)

It's a sweet-tasting herb used in Ayurveda to soothe the throat, improve respiratory health, and support digestion.
