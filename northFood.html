North Indian Cuisine


Chole Bhature

A classic dish from Punjab consisting of spicy chickpea curry (chole) served with deep-fried bread (bhature). It is often eaten for breakfast or lunch.

Palak Paneer

A smooth spinach curry cooked with paneer (Indian cottage cheese), seasoned with spices like cumin, coriander, and garam masala.

Dal Makhani

A rich lentil curry made with whole black lentils (urad dal) and kidney beans, slow-cooked with butter, cream, and spices.

Baingan Bharta

A smoky-flavored dish made from roasted and mashed eggplant, mixed with tomatoes, onions, and spices.

Butter Chicken

A world-renowned dish, butter chicken is marinated chicken cooked in a rich tomato-based gravy with cream and butter.

Rogan Josh

A Kashmiri delicacy, Rogan Josh is a slow-cooked lamb or mutton curry flavored with aromatic spices like cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves.

Kebabs

North India, especially Delhi and Lucknow, is famous for various types of kebabs like Seekh Kebabs, Shami Kebabs, and Galouti Kebabs.

Tandoori Chicken

Chicken marinated in yogurt and spices, then cooked in a tandoor. The result is smoky, tender chicken with a vibrant red color.

Rajma Chawal

Red kidney beans cooked in a tomato-based gravy, spiced with garlic, ginger, and garam masala. It’s served with steamed rice and is a comfort food staple in Punjab.

Amritsari Kulcha

A stuffed bread, often filled with spiced potatoes or paneer, cooked in a tandoor and served with chickpea curry (chole) and pickles.

Makki di Roti and Sarson da Saag

A classic winter dish from Punjab, Makki di Roti is a flatbread made from cornmeal, while Sarson da Saag is a curry made from mustard greens and spices.

Kachori Sabzi

A popular street food in Uttar Pradesh, kachori is a deep-fried pastry filled with spicy lentils, served with potato curry and tamarind chutney.

Dal Baati Churma

A traditional Rajasthani meal that includes baati (hard, baked balls of wheat), served with dal (lentils) and churma (crushed baati mixed with sugar or jaggery and ghee).

Gatte ki Sabzi

This vegetarian dish features gram flour dumplings cooked in a spicy yogurt-based curry.

Kadhi Pakora

This dish consists of deep-fried gram flour fritters (pakoras) served in a tangy yogurt-based gravy.

Chana Madra

A Himachali specialty, this is a yogurt-based chickpea curry cooked with a blend of spices like cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon.
